# Jeremy White - Computer Science ePortfolio

Welcome to my professional ePortfolio for the **CS-499 Computer Science Capstone**
at Southern New Hampshire University. This site presents the work from my
Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, centered on three enhanced artifacts that
demonstrate growth across the core areas of the discipline.

My area of specialization is **applied machine learning and data-driven backend
engineering**, reflecting my professional work on Amazon Flex pricing. The three
artifacts below were chosen and enhanced to showcase that focus: a reinforcement-learning
agent (algorithms), a MongoDB data layer (databases), and a secure, layered mobile
application (software engineering).
